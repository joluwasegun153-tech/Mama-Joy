About Pap & Groundnut

Welcome to Pap & Groundnut, where traditional Nigerian flavours meet quality and care. We are passionate about bringing delicious pap and crunchy groundnuts closer to your home.

Our goal is to make your everyday meals and snacks more enjoyable with products prepared and packaged with care.

OUR STORY

A Tradition Built on Quality and Care

Pap & Groundnut was inspired by the love of traditional Nigerian food. We understand how special a warm bowl of pap and delicious groundnuts can be.

Our goal is to make these traditional favourites conveniently available to customers while focusing on quality, freshness and good service.
